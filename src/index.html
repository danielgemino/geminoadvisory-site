About Us

Independent advice. Strategic impact.

Gemino Advisory is a boutique advisory firm focused on helping organizations strengthen governance, manage risk, and navigate complex compliance expectations with clarity and discipline.

The firm was founded by Daniel Gemino, a governance, compliance, and financial crime risk advisor with more than a decade of experience in international banking, Big Four consulting, and highly regulated environments.
